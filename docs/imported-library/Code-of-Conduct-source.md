# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Echo of Elements respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role in the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with empathy, assume good faith while clarifying misunderstandings, provide actionable and respectful feedback, and focus discussions on the work rather than on individuals. Disagreements are expected in creative and technical work; they should be handled with evidence, curiosity, and a willingness to revise one’s position.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, trolling, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Private information must not be published or shared without explicit permission. Project spaces must not be used to solicit, promote, or distribute unlawful or abusive content.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository’s designated private contact channel. Include enough context to investigate, such as links, dates, messages, or screenshots, while limiting the distribution of sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause or close discussions, request changes, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and keep project spaces productive. Responses will be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing it for this repository.
